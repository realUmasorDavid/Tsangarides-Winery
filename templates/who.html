Who We Are

A family legacy rooted in the vineyards of Lemona, blending tradition with innovation to create wines of character and distinction.

The Tsangarides winery is the next generation of Cypriot wines. Our philosophy is to blend old school traditions with new school style. Our youth, energy, and passion, coupled with the distinct sense of place that wine brings to our lives, allow us to produce wines of great character and distinction.

We are extremely proud to be considered one of the key wineries at the leading edge of the long-awaited revival of Cyprus's wine industry.

"The Tsangarides heritage is one that has enormous respect for the craft as a living breathing art, with the vines being both our livelihood as well as our passion."

The Tsangarides winery is situated in the lovely old village of Lemona. It is here that our great-great-grandfather prepared the land and planted the first vineyards. The knowledge passed down through generations, enriched by travel and collaboration with foreign winemakers, has helped us become one of the best examples of a boutique winery in Cyprus.

Today, The family continue this legacy, carrying the mantle passed on by their father. With deep roots in winemaking running through their veins, they strive to open new "wine frontiers" while facing the challenges of modern winemaking.
