The Vineyard

Set among the hills of Lemona, our vineyards are shaped by altitude, light, and a deep respect for the land that gives each vintage its character.

Where the grapes begin

A landscape of precision and patience

Our vineyard blocks are planted to capture the best balance of sun, breeze, and cool evening air. Every decision, from pruning to harvesting, is made to preserve the natural expression of the fruit and the terroir that surrounds it.

The harvest

From vine to bottle

The vineyard is the heart of our winemaking philosophy. By choosing the right moment for each harvest and handling fruit gently from the first pick to the cellar, we preserve freshness, aroma, and depth in every vintage.
