Founded in 2005, Tsangarides Winery has remained in the family for generations, each one deepening our commitment to the land and to the art of winemaking. Our 100% organic vineyards, nestled in the hills of Paphos, yield grapes of extraordinary character — the foundation of wines that consistently earn the world's highest accolades.
