BECOME PART OF OUR FAMILY

Adopt a Vine

Own a piece of our vineyard. Watch your vine grow, receive the wine made from it, and create lasting memories with every harvest.

HOW IT WORKS

Tsangarides Winery offers you a unique opportunity to adopt your own vine in one of our beautiful organic vineyards in Lemona. Become a part of our story and enjoy the fruits of your own vine for years to come.

01

Your Personal Vine

We plant a plaque with your name (or the name of your loved one) on a selected vine in our vineyard.

02

Annual Wine Allocation

Receive one case (6 bottles) of wine produced from your own vine every year, delivered to your door.

03

Exclusive Experiences

Invitation to harvest your grapes, private tours, 10% discount on all wines, and a beautiful membership certificate.

Perfect as a Gift

Give the gift of wine, nature, and lasting memories. Ideal for birthdays, anniversaries, weddings, or corporate gifts.
